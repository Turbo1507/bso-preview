Company Legal Information

Company details

PT. UNIT NUANU PROPERTY is a limited liability company incorporated and operating under the laws of the Republic of Indonesia.

Registered address: Jl Pantai Nyanyi No. 88, Beraban, Tabanan, Bali 80351, Indonesia.

Activities

The company develops residential and commercial real estate in Bali: design, construction and furnishing of properties, including the UNIT.FURNITURE brand. Black Sands Oasis is being delivered inside the Nuanu cluster on land provided under an agreement with Wooden Fish Village (Nuanu).

Status of the site materials

  • Visualisations may differ from the final built result.
  • The exact characteristics of a unit, timelines and terms of the deal are fixed in the reservation / purchase agreement concluded individually.
  • Prices shown on the site are indicative (prelaunch) and quoted in US dollars for reference; the settlement currency is fixed in the contract.
